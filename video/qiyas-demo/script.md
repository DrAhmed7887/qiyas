# Qiyas demo: voiceover script

Narrator: Gemini TTS (English). Calm, warm, unhurried, about 2.5 words per second. One line per shot, matching storyboard.xml `<voiceover>` line for line. Pronounce Qiyas as "kee-YAHS".


## open

- **open-1** (0–6s, 11 words): At home, a toddler might be measured standing against a wall.
- **open-2** (6–12s, 12 words): But WHO measures children under two lying down. Qiyas checks the difference.

## correction

- **corr-1** (12–18s, 12 words): A synthetic example: a boy, 607 days old, 78 centimetres, measured standing.
- **corr-2** (18–26s, 14 words): Measured as entered, length for age reads z minus 2.19: below the usual range.
- **corr-3** (26–33s, 17 words): WHO adds 0.7 centimetres for standing under two. Now it reads minus 1.94, in the usual range.
- **corr-4** (33–37s, 5 words): Same child. Same tape measure.

## language

- **lang-1** (37–44s, 9 words): Switch to English. Every number stays exactly the same.

## safety

- **safe-1** (44–51s, 14 words): Now a harder case. The weight entered is implausible, so Qiyas shows no number.
- **safe-2** (51–59s, 18 words): But the parent answered yes: swelling of both feet. That is a warning sign, whatever the numbers say.
- **safe-3** (59–65s, 14 words): So the first thing on screen is: see a health worker today. No diagnosis.
- **safe-4** (65–69s, 9 words): A typical child: no alarm. Keep up regular check-ups.

## ai

- **ai-1** (69–80s, 23 words): One optional AI feature answers a fixed question from the approved text. It sits below the result and is not wired to it.

## proof

- **proof-1** (80–88s, 12 words): The data is WHO's own expanded tables, downloaded from WHO, values unmodified.
- **proof-2** (88–98s, 21 words): The tests feed WHO's own table rows back through the engine and expect minus three to plus three. 134 tests pass.
- **proof-3** (98–106s, 17 words): Bands and the urgent action come from WHO cut-offs in plain code. No AI in the maths.

## scope

- **scope-1** (106–114s, 11 words): Shipped: three WHO indicators, ages zero to five, Arabic and English.
- **scope-2** (114–121s, 11 words): Not shipped: BMI, school-age children, tracking over visits, a native app.

## close

- **close-1** (121–129s, 18 words): WHO did the math. Parents need the meaning. Credit to WHO Anthro and UNICEF's Bebbo, who came first.
